4. Methodology & Notes

  • Data Sources: OTD.csv (CURR/UPDT speed values per store-month), 5-Star.csv (overall scores, speed stars), Alignment.csv (store-to-franchisee mapping).
  • Speed Star Buckets: 1 (0–34.99), 2 (35–43.99), 3 (44–52.99), 4 (53–69.99), 5 (70–100). CURR and UPDT values (stored as decimals in OTD) are multiplied by 100 for bucket assignment.
  • 5-Star Overall Score Formula: Win Score 35% + Speed Score 30% + HB Ontime 20% + FSCC 7.5% + Brand 7.5%.
  • Tier Boundaries: Tier 1 (1.0 – 2.5), Tier 2 (2.5 – 4.0), Tier 3 (4.0+).
  • Improvement Impact: Moving up one speed star bucket adds 0.30 points to the Overall Score (30% weight × 1 star).
  • “Updated” Scenario: Calculated as UpdatedOverall = CurrentOverall + (UpdtBucket − CurrBucket) × 0.3. Only the speed component changes; all other component scores (win score, brand, etc.) remain as in the source 5-Star data.
  • 471 store-months (1.7%) had no matching 5-Star record and are excluded from tier analysis.
