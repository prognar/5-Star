Executive Summary

This analysis examines 4,590 stores across January through June 2026, comparing each store's current speed score (CURR) against an updated value (UPDT) to determine how many would cross into a higher speed star bucket. A speed bucket improvement adds 0.30 points to the Overall 5-Star Score (30% weight), which can shift a store into a higher tier — Tier 1 (scores 1–2.5), Tier 2 (2.5–4), or Tier 3 (4+).

1 in 4 store-months showed a speed bucket improvement: 6,652 changes out of 27,453 store-months (24.2%). The rate is consistent across the six months, ranging from 22.7% in February to 25.1% in May, with a slight upward drift as the year progressed. Of those improvements, 1,315 store-months would have moved up an entire tier — 611 from Tier 1 to Tier 2 and 704 from Tier 2 to Tier 3. This means Tier 1 would shrink by roughly 110 stores per month, while Tier 3 would grow by a similar margin.

The impact varies significantly by franchisee. Top performers like James Gressett (34%), Ed Herron (32.7%), and Charan Dhaliwal (31.5%) see roughly one in three store-months improve, while the system average sits at 24%. Large operators see meaningful absolute impact — Greg Flynn (949 stores) recorded 271 tier moves, Jason Cochran (179 stores) had 74, and Shoukat Dhanani (346 stores) had 103. Stores on the Tier 2/3 boundary are the most likely to cross the 4.0 threshold with a single bucket move, making them the highest-leverage targets for speed-focused coaching.

The key takeaway: even a modest speed improvement that crosses one bucket boundary has a measurable impact on a store's Overall 5-Star Score and tier standing. For stores near a tier boundary, the uplift can meaningfully improve incentive qualification, brand perception, and operational ratings.

5. Methodology & Notes

  • Data Sources: OTD.csv (CURR/UPDT speed values per store–month), 5-Star.csv (overall scores, speed stars), Alignment.csv (store-to-franchisee mapping).
  • Speed Star Buckets: 1 (0–34.99), 2 (35–43.99), 3 (44–52.99), 4 (53–69.99), 5 (70–100). CURR/UPDT values (decimals in OTD) are multiplied by 100 for bucket assignment.
  • 5-Star Overall Score Formula: Win Score 35% + Speed Score 30% + HB Ontime 20% + FSCC 7.5% + Brand 7.5%.
  • Tier Boundaries: Tier 1 (1.0 – 2.5), Tier 2 (2.5 – 4.0), Tier 3 (4.0+).
  • Improvement Impact: Moving up one speed star bucket adds 0.30 points to the Overall Score (30% weight × 1 star). UpdatedOverall = CurrentOverall + (UpdtBucket − CurrBucket) × 0.3
  • Scope: Only the speed component changes. Win Score, Brand, HB Ontime, and FSCC remain at their source values.
  • 471 store–months (1.7%) had no matching 5-Star record and are excluded from tier analysis.
